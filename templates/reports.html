System Methodology

1. Data Ingestion: Ingests high-volume retail transactions, automatically mapping arbitrary schemas.

2. RFM Modeling: Aggregates transactions into 3 behavioral dimensions: Recency (days), Frequency (visits), Monetary (spend).

3. Skew Handling: Applies \( \log(1 + x) \) to handle heavy right-skewed sales distributions.

4. Unsupervised Clustering: Deploys K-Means with K-Means++ centroid initialization and Silhouette score evaluation.

5. Recommendation Engine: Maps cluster centroids to distinct commercial strategies with actionable promotional tactics.
