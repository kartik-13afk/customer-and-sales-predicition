Recency (R)

Days elapsed since the customer's most recent completed order.

Formula: Snapshot Date - Max(InvoiceDate)
Lower days = Higher engagement = R_Score 5
Frequency (F)

Total distinct purchase visits / orders placed by the customer.

Formula: Count(Distinct Invoices)
Higher orders = Habitual buyer = F_Score 5
Monetary (M)

Total cumulative gross revenue generated by the customer.

Formula: Sum(Quantity * UnitPrice)
Higher spend = High commercial value = M_Score 5
